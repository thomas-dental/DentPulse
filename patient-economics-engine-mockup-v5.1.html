Design mockup · aligned build v5.1 (API-audited, terminology-clean). DentPulse Enterprise “Patient Economics Engine” for the multi-practice manager. v5.1 extends the frozen v5 baseline with a per-treatment P&L on the Patient Financial Record: the directory now shows Revenue (Dentally) − Cost (attributed) = Contribution (Derived) as three columns, and each row expands to the completed-treatment breakdown, with patient-level overheads (membership service, CAC) shown as a separate deduction line so the lines reconcile to the totals. v5 purges the residual “committed / not booked” language left over from v3, which contradicted the Scheduled model: opportunity states are now observable (Planned-unscheduled = commercial opportunity, Completed-not-charged = billing leakage, Charged-not-collected = cash leakage). Commitment Rate™ is kept as the branded KPI (% of eligible planned private treatment value that becomes Scheduled within N days) sitting on the raw Scheduled event; the Settings “Scheduling window” defines N. Confidence is a four-tier provenance model (Dentally / Derived / Modelled / External) with an overall score; mixed-provenance tiles now split their tags (avg contribution Derived, projected LTV Modelled). Growth levers are named for dentistry: Visit Frequency × Value per Visit × Patient Lifetime → Patient Economic Value. Collected = recorded in the PMS, not bank-settled. Per-clinician remuneration profiles; NHS/UDA tracked apart via nhs_uda_value / NHS Claims; conversion learned from history; Recovery Loop is DentPulse accountability over Dentally evidence; CAC attribution next version. All names and figures are illustrative placeholders.
